How To Read It

1. One card = one physical strip.
2. `Patch parts` = how that strip is split into universes.
3. Preview always shows the whole strip in one line.
4. `PART ON/OFF` checks one universe part, `DIR ON/OFF` checks physical start/end, `RGB TEST` checks color order.

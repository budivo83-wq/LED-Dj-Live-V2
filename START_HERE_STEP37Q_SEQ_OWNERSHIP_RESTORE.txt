STEP37Q - Sequencer FX/FLIP ownership restore

Base: STEP37P_MAPPING_LIVE_PATCH_SAFE_SEQ_BOOT

Changes:

- When the sequencer FX cell ends, the previous manual FX state is restored instead of forcing FX off.
- Quick step sequencer now remembers previous manual layer flip state before it applies a sequencer FLIP cell.
- When the sequencer FLIP cell ends, the previous manual flip state is restored instead of forcing reverse off.
- Turning the sequencer OFF now releases only sequencer-owned momentary FX/FLIP states through the same restore path.

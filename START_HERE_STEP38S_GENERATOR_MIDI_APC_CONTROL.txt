STEP38S — Generator MIDI / APC Control

Start here:
1. Open Generator.
2. Open panel "08 MIDI / APC Control".
3. Click CONNECT MIDI.
4. Select your APC/Oxygen/generic MIDI input, or leave AUTO.
5. Click MIDI ON.
6. Move CC 16–23 or CC 48–55 for Macro 1–8.
7. Press pads/notes 36–42 for music modes, 52 for blackout, 53 for panic release, 54 for LIVE toggle.

Learn mode:
- Pick an action in Learn action.
- Click LEARN NEXT.
- Move a knob or press a pad.
- Mapping is saved in localStorage.

Safety:
- MIDI does not create any new UDP/ArtNet writer.
- LIVE toggle still uses the existing Generator LIVE MASTER takeover/crossfade path.
- Blackout publishes physically only when Generator is already LIVE MASTER.

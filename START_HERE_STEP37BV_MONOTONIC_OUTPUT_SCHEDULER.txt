STEP37BV – MONOTONIC OUTPUT SCHEDULER + LOW-HOTPATH METRICS

Cil:
- stabilizovat fyzicky output frame loop,
- omezit mikro-jitter z opakovaneho armovani setTimeout,
- nebudit debug/UI metriky v enqueue hot path.

Upraveno:
- public/runtime/output_pipeline.js

Zmeny:
1) Single monotonic scheduler
   - pridan schedulerActive + nextWakeAt,
   - planovani je centralizovane pres scheduleNextWake(),
   - timeout se pocita vzdy proti performance.now()/getNow,
   - po pause/overload se scheduler sam preplanuje bez zbytecneho re-arm chaosu.

2) Enqueue bez metrickeho wake-upu
   - pri kazdem prichozim frame se uz hned nepousti emitMetrics(),
   - frame se jen ulozi do single-slot fronty a probudi scheduler,
   - dropped/disabled/error udalosti zustavaji reportovane.

3) Idle path bez zbytecnych metrics eventu
   - drain() bez queuedFrame uz nepali metrics event,
   - metriky zustavaji dostupne pres getMetrics() a throttlovany emit po skutecne praci.

4) Diagnostika
   - metrics.version = 37BV-monotonic-scheduler,
   - pridano schedulerActive a schedulerDelayMs.

Ocekavany efekt:
- mene mikro-zaseku ve fyzickem outputu,
- mensi main-thread tlak pri 60 FPS,
- mene CustomEvent/telemetry prace mimo skutecne potrebne snapshoty.

Kontrola:
- node --check probehl na 477 JS/MJS souborech bez chyby.

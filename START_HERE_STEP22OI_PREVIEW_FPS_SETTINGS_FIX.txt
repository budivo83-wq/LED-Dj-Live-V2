Oprava:
- Settings ma novou/aktivni kolonku PREVIEW FPS.
- Hodnota previewFps se uklada do led-engine-settings-v1.
- Stejna hodnota se zapisuje i do live preview cap klicu:
  - budilightLivePreviewFpsCap
  - budilightShellLivePreviewFpsCap
- app.js pri startu prepisuje RUNTIME_PROFILE.previewFps podle ulozene hodnoty.
- fps_governor cte previewFps dynamicky z localStorage.
- render_pipeline uz nepouziva natvrdo 6/10 FPS pri live outputu; visible shell/live preview jede podle Settings -> PREVIEW FPS.

Doporuceni pro test:
1) Settings -> PREVIEW FPS nastav napr. 20 nebo 30.
2) Uloz/reloadni appku.
3) Output ArtNet nech na 60 FPS.
4) Kdyz UI zacne cukat, sniz PREVIEW FPS, ne output FPS.
